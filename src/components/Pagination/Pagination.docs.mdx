## Anatomy & Architecture

The pagination pattern is organized in three composable tiers:

1. **Base Primitives**:
   - `PageNumber`: 24px/36px circular or square page buttons with 4 interaction states (`inactive`, `hover`, `active v1`, `active v2`).
   - `TruncatedPages`: Ellipsis spacer (`…`) indicating hidden page intervals.
   - `PageIndicator`: Formatted text status (`"Page 1 of 30"` or `"Showing 1–10 of 100 entries"`).
   - `NavigationControls`: Prev/next triggers in 4 presentation types (`single buttons`, `grouped buttons`, `button`, `selector`) across 4 color treatments (`outlined`, `filled-brand`, `filled-dark`, `ghost`).

2. **Composite Row**:
   - `PaginationList`: An auto-layout row combining page numbers and ellipsis tokens with 2px gap and optional directional arrows.

3. **Full Navigation Bar**:
   - `Pagination`: Complete responsive bar supporting top divider rules, 5 Figma layout variations, and viewport alignments.
